A mini Siri for your Windows desktop. It lives in a small black notch at the top of your screen, listens when you ask, and answers out loud.

How it works.

  1. 01

    Wake it

    Press Alt + J, or tap the notch.

  2. 02

    Speak

    Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Say it the way you would say it to a person.

  3. 03

    It thinks

    Weather comes live from Open-Meteo. Everything else goes to Claude, with web search.

  4. 04

    It answers

    Aloud, in a British butler voice.

Set up in a minute.

No installer, no account. Bring your own keys.

Download the zip
  1. Download Jarvis-win.zip.
  2. Unzip it anywhere you like.
  3. Run Jarvis.exe. The notch appears at the top of your screen.
  4. Right-click the notch and choose Set keys. This opens %APPDATA%\Jarvis\keys.json.
{
  "anthropic": "",
  "groq": "",
  "deepgram": "",
  "elevenlabs": "",
  "voice": "",
  "model": "",
  "city": "New Delhi",
  "name": "sir"
}

Fill in your own keys and save. voice is an ElevenLabs voice id, model a Claude model id (leave it empty for the default), city your home city for weather, and name what Jarvis calls you. Weather works with no keys at all.

Your keys never leave your PC. They stay in that one file.
